AI for survival
and quality of life

I develop AI methods to support clinical and patient decisions under uncertainty. My long-term goal is to maximise survival and quality of life across the care journey.

The research vision

Evidence. Decisions.
Consequences over time.

My work connects explainability, uncertainty estimation, and sequential decision-making. The scientific challenge is to use incomplete evidence to anticipate the consequences of actions and support decisions across the patient journey.
