Research perspective

What changes when AI becomes part of a decision?

Predictive performance is one part of the question. My research examines how people interpret AI outputs, how uncertainty should inform reliance, and how sequences of actions can support longer-term outcomes.

I develop and evaluate methods for clinical decision support and personalized digital health, with a focus on cancer care. This work connects treatment planning and shared decision-making with patient support and adherence.
