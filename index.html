Aneta Lisowska · Assistant Professor in AI and Health

AI for survival
and quality of life

My research aims to maximise survival and quality of life by developing AI that helps clinicians and patients make decisions from uncertain evidence across the care journey.

Explore my research ↗About me ↗

The research vision

Evidence. Decisions.
Consequences over time.

I connect uncertainty-aware patient-state estimation, action-conditioned transition models, and explainable sequential decisions. The aim is to evaluate what AI at different stages of care contributes to survival and quality of life.
