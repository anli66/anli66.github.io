AI that makes a difference in cancer care.

Research with people at its heart

Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam · Cancer Center Amsterdam UMC

From data to decisions.
From decisions to better care.

My research investigates how artificial intelligence can improve decision-making in cancer care.

I build and test AI tools with a dual focus: supporting clinicians with treatment planning and shared decision-making, and helping patients with treatment adherence. My goal is to understand how these tools influence choices and to work towards measurable improvements in survival and quality of life.
